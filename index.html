🧭 Prepositions & Case Governance
Rules for which prepositions take the Accusative vs Ablative cases.

Takes Ablative (SID SPACE)

S - sub (under, at rest)
I - in (in, on, at rest)
D - de (down from, about)
S - sine (without)
P - pro (in front of, on behalf of)
A - a / ab (away from, by)
C - cum (with)
E - e / ex (out of, from)

Takes Accusative (Motion Towards)

ad (to, towards)
ante (before, in front of)
circum (around)
contra (against)
in (into, onto - with motion)
inter (among, between)
per (through, along)
post (after, behind)
prope (near)
trans (across)
